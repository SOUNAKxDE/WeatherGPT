Four inseparable pillars

Every feature in WeatherGPT lives inside one of these — from a citizen's umbrella question to a command centre's rescue route.

Weather Intelligence

Real-time observations, forecasts, radar, satellite and environmental conditions — accessible by conversation.

Predictive Intelligence

NWP fusion, nowcasting, thunderstorm and lightning tracking, heavy-rainfall and inundation probability.

Impact Intelligence

Exposure, infrastructure effects, cascading hazard chains, route, shelter and resource implications.

Response Intelligence

Alerts, command dashboards, mesh-resilient SOS, incident prioritization and rescue routing.

One assistant, every kind of weather question

Conversational core

Ask naturally, in text or voice, in English or regional languages. Follow-up questions carry context.

Nowcasting

15/30/60/120-minute thunderstorm, lightning and rainfall projections with confidence bands.

Flood & inundation

Rainfall + terrain + drainage + river level fusion to estimate extent, depth and time-to-impact.

Cascading risk

Cyclone → rainfall → flood → road blockage → power/network loss, modeled as a live graph.

Mesh SOS

Battery-aware emergency signalling that relays through nearby devices when networks fail.

Command centre

Hazard, impact, incident and resource views for authorized disaster-management officers.
